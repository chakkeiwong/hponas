# DOC-01 historical features and product documentation audit

Revision 2.519 is the immutable completed baseline. DOC-01 is separately
tracked. Completion of the adopted finite research scope is not implementation
of every proposal idea, nor production readiness for a retail bank.

## Findings and dispositions

1. **Documentation omission:** August LaTeX describes an unbuilt proposal and
   lacks installation, executable APIs, compatible methods, recovery, later
   bank profiles, RGPE and local service repairs. DOC-01/MANUAL places a current
   manual before the preserved, explicitly historical survey.
2. **API drift:** proposal interfaces and Ray Tune substrate are conceptual
   history. Study adapts native suggest/observe and supported legacy proposal
   interfaces; LocalExecutor and Ray Core serve declared workflows. Legacy Tune
   compatibility is separate.
3. **Historical scope change:** PPO power/pilot plans became self-contained
   finite known-answer validation. Eight studies/four local workers do not
   establish original 100-study/50-worker production targets, remote fault
   tolerance or hostile-tenant security. These are unqualified capabilities.
4. **Method status differs from roadmap disposition:** PBT, PB2-Mix, BG-PBT,
   DEHB and narrow robust GP exist despite historical deferred labels. RGPE is
   now an actual fixed-kernel ensemble distinct from ranked initialization.
   Robust GP refuses repeated declared Study where failure support is absent.
5. **Terminal unsuccessful outcome:** ifBO feasibility failed its frozen rule;
   integration was cancelled. SAASBO, SMAC, FIRE-PBT, MF-PBT, IPBT, SEARL, MORBO,
   HVKG, general PFNs, CARBS and scaling-law methods remain watchlists. General
   topology, supernet and zero-cost NAS remain excluded. Accounted-for does not
   mean implemented.
6. **Application evidence gap:** synthetic workflows do not prove real-bank
   accuracy, arbitrary-data causal identification, posterior convergence,
   profits or optimizer superiority. DOC-01 adds bounded demonstrations, not
   those broader claims.
7. **NAS demonstration gap:** existing solver/readout fixtures construct hidden
   features and fit readouts. DOC-01 adds actual gradient training across neural
   depth and width, with an independent equilibrium/policy derivative reference.
8. **Governance defect exposed by extension:** the existing final gap guard
   requires GAP-01/FINAL-AUDIT to remain last. Adding stages after it failed
   checks. Preserve that guard and reopen terminal closure after DOC-01;
   qualified scientific source remains unchanged.
9. **Freshness:** version 0.1.0 still has Alpha metadata. Immutable evidence
   contains contemporaneous open-qualification prose; later package and final
   audit close those states. Do not rewrite that historical evidence. Current
   guidance must point to the latest package and explain scope and lineage.
