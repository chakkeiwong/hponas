## Reviewed design boundaries

- The generator is exactly the retained uniform two-covariate synthetic law;
  model constants are the exact saved binary64 values. The certified policy
  uses real arithmetic and deterministic lowest-index ties. This is not a
  floating-point implementation error certificate or real-bank evidence.
- CDF values need outward enclosures: a convergent integral series with a
  proved remainder and monotone endpoint bounds, or a comparably justified
  interval algorithm. Ordinary scipy quadrature and agreement of refinements
  are insufficient. Arithmetic assumptions and remainder derivation are recorded.
- A full binary partition covers [-1,1]^2. Possible-action sets include all
  maximizers, including ties. Utility difference bounds exploit identical fixed
  actions exactly; correlations are never used to narrow bounds without proof.
- The installable add-on is chosen because these tools verify optional finite
  scientific claims and do not alter search behavior. Adding them directly to
  core would change its fingerprint and require broader requalification. The
  design does not alter source_identity or relabel stale acceptance. The add-on
  must be usable without imports from sibling repositories or research scripts.
- Retain consumed SCI-01/02 source, failures, and evidence unchanged. The six
  failed ifBO comparisons, unknown-MNAR/causal nonidentification and limits on
  sampler/NAS generality remain explicit. Real-bank effectiveness requires
  a named data/estimand contract; it cannot be manufactured by this repair.

## Bounded execution and phase repair

One inspector/editor; one CPU/thread; no GPU, network, services, training fits,
sampler chains, fresh observations or pretrained forwards. Each new operation
has a reviewed hash-bound single-use manifest and fixed roster. Wallet and
installed saved-artifact verification each have at most 600 seconds; delivery
build and final audit each at most 300 seconds. A manifest may split installed
NAS verification from build to respect these bounds. Maximum owned RSS 2 GiB,
output 64 MiB, minimum initial disk 2 GiB. The optional package is built offline
from local tools; no dependency download or global environment modification.

At phase boundaries retain results, repair demonstrated defects with a reviewed
successor allocation, refresh the next phase and continue. A budget exhaustion
is a valid incomplete numerical result, not scientific success. Do not run a
broad campaign or keep retrying for favorable signs. Exact operations are added
to docs/PROGRAM_OPERATIONS.md and its hash reconciled with archived predecessor
bytes. No global allow-rule edits, git-history changes or deployment.
