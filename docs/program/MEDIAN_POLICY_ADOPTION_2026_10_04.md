revision 1.61 corrects the requirement ownership and completion wording.

shipping as the default is conditional on a PASS under the registered V06

performance result is inferred here. Endpoint-only demonstrations do not close

release prerequisite. These assignments preserve both obligations without a
Tier-0 to Tier-1 to Tier-0 dependency cycle. Policy acceptance is not evidence
of performance, Tier-0 release, or automatic default activation.
