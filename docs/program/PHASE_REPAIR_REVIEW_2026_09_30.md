`tier0_rank_sizing.py` implements a distribution-free sufficient statistical
power bound. For independent `X_i` in `[-a,b]`, set
`Y_i=(X_i+a)/(a+b)`. Concavity lower-bounds each log betting factor by the
chord between its endpoints. If `sum Y_i >= k`, the registered mixture exceeds
its value for `k` upper endpoints and `n-k` lower endpoints. Convexity of the
negative-exponential moment and AM-GM give the Chernoff bound
`P(sum Y_i < k) <= exp(-n KL(k/n || p))` when
`p <= average E[Y_i]` and `p > k/n`. Thus `1-exp(-n KL(...))` is a sufficient
component power. Eight V04 components combine by a dependence-safe union
bound; each entry uses .025, sufficient for the two-entry Holm family.
An all-upper-endpoint alternative is handled exactly. The accepted .80 target,
zero superiority margin, 20-vector cap and fixed count remain unchanged.

Anytime lower utilities use support `[-1,.99]` on the current grid: missing
records may retain -1, whereas the initial time interval cannot contribute a
positive win. Tests compare the bound to exact ternary and nonidentical binary
laws, not just the implementation's own formula. The executable frontier takes
subseconds and computes conditional counts; it never claims that actual PPO
means satisfy the assumed alternatives. Operational loss/cost vetoes can only
reduce campaign power, so their probability also needs evidence. At fixed n,
binary-law rejection probability is continuous at the null: uniformly .80
power against every arbitrarily small positive effect is impossible. A failed
sufficient bound is not itself an impossibility proof for a particular effect.
