## Reviewed corrections to the proposed approach

* CV cannot be guaranteed to improve each sample. Repair decision-objective alignment, finite-space duplication and honest uncertainty rather than chase a universal CV win.
* Missing-not-at-random identification cannot be repaired by more optimization. Provide an explicit observational-equivalence witness and sharp bounded-outcome intervals; refuse point identification without assumptions.
* All prespecified NAS replicates passing is a finite confirmation criterion, not a theorem about all seeds or the continuous domain. Keep original derivative limits and preserve failures.
* ESS, MCSE and R-hat are assessment diagnostics, not convergence proofs and not candidate-ranking objectives for the native inference contract. Freeze candidate policies before posterior draws; compare gradient budgets honestly.
* A real-workload adapter is a separate data/identification contract. Here repair representative standalone mechanisms and public Study integration. Do not create a forbidden sibling dependency.
* A completed operation is not automatically a successful repair. Report mechanics_passed separately from scientific_status and keep failed claims in a visible outcome ledger.
