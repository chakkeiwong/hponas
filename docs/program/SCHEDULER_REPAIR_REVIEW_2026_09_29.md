## Implemented contract

Default scalar ASHA now implements the pinned Syne Tune 0.13.0 promotion policy: at least two observations, a linear `1 / eta` cutoff, eligibility at ties, highest-rung priority, and one dispatch per scheduling request. Promoted observations remain in the reference population; waiting trials remain paused and can qualify after later arrivals. Objective-direction normalization belongs to Study. Exact duplicate reports are idempotent, conflicting duplicate metrics are rejected, and stale events cannot revive a terminal trial.

The old bootstrap/quota policy and live-point median heuristic remain explicitly available as `legacy_bootstrap`. Its historical tests and replay fixtures now name that policy. Those checks are not evidence of reference-policy conformance. Reference checkpoints carry policy identity and reject an implicit legacy restore. Invalid scheduler recovery is validated on an isolated candidate before replacing live state.

Objectives can declare a reporter. Study processes their messages on its own thread while local threads, local timeout subprocesses, or Ray workers wait for scheduling decisions. This keeps scheduler transitions, SQLite, checkpoints, and replay order together. Completion, pruning, failure, cancellation, and a successful paused rung are distinguished. Full training enters the completed-reference population only after a successful final evaluation; merely finishing a rung is insufficient. Replay now recomputes streamed and terminal transitions and rejects inconsistent event identities, controls, and completion outcomes.

Median pruning uses arithmetic means over a declared report-grid prefix, comparing the current trial with the median of completed trials' matching prefix means. It requires five usable completed references by default. Missing steps are not interpolated, and running/paused/pruned/failed/cancelled trials do not become completed references. Controls and histories survive JSON recovery. Endpoint-only objectives remain executable but do not claim streamed median conformance.

Promotions preserve root/evaluation/parent identity and declare restart or resume behavior. Resume requires a durable JSON checkpoint/reference and an objective accepting the continuation context. Restart pays for repeated work, which does not become new scheduler evidence. Study's configuration count is separate from its measured elapsed-seconds cap. If no incumbent reaches the required fidelity, `IncompleteStudyError.result` and the durable Store/checkpoint expose an incomplete outcome; no lower-fidelity result substitutes for it.

- **Compute accounting:** the elapsed cap is cooperative and can overshoot within an in-flight report interval or endpoint-only call. It does not measure active accelerator time. A driver crash retains acknowledged work but cannot reconstruct an unreported suffix; recovered evaluations expose `lower_bound_after_interruption`. Such data cannot certify exact compute savings.

They do not establish full vendor-distribution equivalence, the final 10,000-case V02 population, real-workload superiority, mutation acceptance, or multi-node operations. The optional prior-performance failures remain unchanged and separately recorded under PRI-01.
