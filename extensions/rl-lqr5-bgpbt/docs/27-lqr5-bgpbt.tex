\chapter{Five-state joint architecture and SAC tuning}
\label{sec:lqr5-bgpbt}

This chapter extends the scalar example with the optional
\texttt{hponas\_rl\_bgpbt} companion. The original finite-MDP
\texttt{LocalBGPBT} API and its accepted source remain unchanged. The companion
uses RLlib's upstream Torch SAC learner, explicit sampling and uniform replay,
the repository's temporal BG-PBT Gaussian process, and isolated resource workers.
The master stage is RL-LQR-03; its current completion state is governed by the
structured program register, including the separate GPU requirement.

\section{Known-answer model and joint search space}
The dynamics are $x_{t+1}=Ax_t+Bu_t+w_t$, with five states, one action,
$A=0.8I+0.12C$ for a cyclic permutation $C$, $B=e_1$, $|u_t|\leq1$,
horizon eight and discount $\gamma=0.99$. State and terminal costs use $I$;
the action cost is $0.1u_t^2$. Initial coordinates are independent signs times
0.5; each innovation is one independent sign times
$0.025(1,-1,1,-1,1)^\top$. Thus the initial covariance is full rank while
instantaneous innovation covariance has rank one. All $2^{13}=8192$ paths
are enumerated. The observation includes the five states and remaining time.

Matrix Riccati recursion and independent block quadratic elimination agree.
The optimal cost is 3.2540811593967556, zero-action cost is 3.889419041418345,
and maximum oracle action magnitude is 0.562377, proving attainability under
the action bound. For every scored deterministic policy the evaluator verifies
the performance-difference identity
\[
 J(\pi)-J^*=\mathbb{E}\sum_{t=0}^{7}\gamma^t
 (u_t+K_tx_t)^\top(R+\gamma B^\top P_{t+1}B)(u_t+K_tx_t).
\]
Identical float32 observations are evaluated once and their actions reused for
all duplicate histories. This prevents matrix-kernel position rounding from
introducing an artificial dependence on future path signs. No numerical
identity tolerance was relaxed to repair this issue.

Actor and critic independently use two, three or four actual hidden layers;
each layer independently has an integer width from 8 through 64. Twin critics
share an architecture specification but have independent weights and targets.
The encoder/head split adds no extra hidden layer. There are
115,443,055,380,249 actor/critic architecture pairs. Actor and critic learning
rates are independently continuous in $[10^{-5},10^{-2}]$ on a logarithmic scale.
Activation is tanh; temperature rate is fixed at $3\times10^{-4}$.

\section{Population, transfer and resource semantics}
The original pilot sequence below is retained for provenance. The subsequent
selection review introduced \texttt{JointExplorer} and
\texttt{population\_search\_v2}. The successor uses an objective-based
architecture surrogate with Bayesian and random proposals, one trust-region
update per population round, and a global-GP restart that clears local history.
All four candidate architectures receive 64 imitation updates followed by SAC
at their proposed rates before objective-based halving. It retains the best
evaluated checkpoint for both methods. The pilot's static confirmation and the
successor's complete procedure replications answer different questions.
RLlib supplies the SAC loss, reparameterized Gaussian actor, two Huber-loss
critics, temperature optimizer and Polyak targets. The companion supplies
uniform one-step replay of capacity 32768, batch 128, 256 initial random
transitions, and one SAC update per four subsequent transitions. One SAC step
updates four optimizer components. The score is deterministic unregularized
quadratic cost; entropy regularization is solely a training mechanism. Oracle
gains and oracle actions never enter training or distillation.

The temporal mixed Mat\'ern GP models cost improvement with time, initial cost,
architectures and two log-rate coordinates. Depths are categorical and inactive
width slots have a canonical value. Within generations, bounded continuous
rate proposals use an adaptive trust region and UCB; collapse triggers a global
restart. Between generations, 64 fresh conditional configurations are proposed
and a finite UCB batch selects four. Optimizer convergence status is retained.
This is neither exhaustive architecture enumeration nor a proof of globally
maximal acquisition.

The original pilot uses four members and four training segments.
Compatible exploit events after segments one and three copy full training
state before applying rate changes and forking future child random streams.
An age-triggered generation after segment two distills four candidates for
16 steps each, keeps two by training-replay distillation loss, and gives each
48 further steps. Distillation matches the pre-squash Gaussian by analytical
KL and matches both soft-Q functions on recorded training actions. The immutable
teacher is checked by scientific-state digest. Targets are hard-copied from
the resulting online critics, incompatible Adam states are reset, temperature
value is retained, and replay is copied only for the identical training contract.
All 160 distillation steps are charged, including discarded candidates.

Checkpoints carry actor, critics, targets, four Adam states, temperature,
target-update clock, replay, environment state/time, and Torch, NumPy, Python,
environment and replay randomness. CPU uninterrupted/resumed continuation is
tested exactly. Task intents precede dispatch; committed results replay without
training, while an unresolved intent is charged and refuses automatic retry.
The controller reconstructs proposals and lineage from committed results and
its seed. Full controller replay is tested to leave the work ledger unchanged.
GPU workers receive one visible physical device before importing Torch. Devices
may be shared with other processes: admission requires 4 GiB free, a 1 GiB
Torch allocator cap and a monitored 2 GiB owned process limit per worker.
Utilization is a placement preference, not an idle-device prerequisite.

\section{Prospective sequence and measured results}
Two fixed calibration seeds precede search. Costs are measured at 0, 2048,
4096 and 8192 transitions. The frozen rule chooses 8192 transitions per member
if the final median improves from 4096, otherwise 4096. This allocates computation;
it is not a human-selected learning-success threshold. A static random-search
baseline starts with the same four configurations and seeds. Both methods use
the same transition count and component-gradient minibatch count: 120 extra
baseline SAC steps match $160\times3$ distillation component updates. Equal
minibatch counts do not imply equal FLOPs or wall time.

Fresh seeds 801, 802 and 803 are reserved before search. Confirmation trains
each selected final static configuration from scratch, rather than replaying
the evolved lineage. It estimates the quality of those static selections;
the full selected lineage is retained as a separate artifact. All costs,
variation and unfavorable comparisons are reported. Three seeds cannot prove
universal reliability or global HPO/NAS optimality. The Riccati gap remains an
objective suboptimality certificate for each saved policy on this finite law.

\section{User interface and limitations}
The companion guide is
\texttt{extensions/rl-lqr5-bgpbt/README.md}.
\texttt{SACMember} provides construction, \texttt{train}, \texttt{score},
\texttt{save}, \texttt{restore} and \texttt{compatible\_copy}.
The separate \texttt{distill} function changes shapes;
\texttt{Explorer} provides proposals and serialized search state.
\texttt{population\_search} executes the reviewed four-member sequence.
The retained pilot API is supplemented by \texttt{population\_search\_v2}
for the repaired objective-based procedure.
\texttt{run\_task} provides write-ahead task accounting and works with
\texttt{ResourceExecutor}. Checkpoints are trusted local Torch files.

The fixed operation runner is \texttt{program\_operations/sac\_bgpbt\_ops.py},
with engineering, repair-engineering, procedure-check, calibration, search, confirmation, hardware, documentation,
audit and master-audit operations. Exact standalone commands and limits appear
in \texttt{docs/PROGRAM\_OPERATIONS.md}. Every operation needs its active master
stage and source-bound manifest. Successful scientific allocations cannot be
silently repeated. The implementation is a bounded local companion for the
declared five-state contract, not an arbitrary-environment production service
or a new qualification of the core finite-MDP implementation. Cross-device
CPU/CUDA random-trajectory equality is not claimed.
