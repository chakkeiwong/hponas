\chapter{Multi-action joint SAC tuning}
\label{sec:lqr-multi-action}

\section{What is the action network?}
RL-LQR-05 retains ten physical states and thirty time steps and varies the
number of control inputs $m\in\{2,3,4,5\}$. Control inputs are the action
vector, distinct from the eleven observation features (state and time).
The actor is the action network: eleven inputs pass through two to four tanh
hidden layers, each having eight to sixty-four neurons, then a $2m$-output
Gaussian head. It emits $m$ means and $m$ log standard deviations during
training. Exported deterministic actions are $\tanh(\mu)\in[-1,1]^m$.
Each of two independent critics takes eleven observation values plus $m$
actions and outputs one soft-Q value. The critics share a selected layer
layout, not weights. Actor and critic layouts are searched independently
with their respective learning rates in $[10^{-5},10^{-2}]$ on log scale.
The architecture/rate candidate is evaluated only after real SAC training.

\section{Fixed model family and analytical comparison}
For each $m$, $B=[e_1,\ldots,e_m]$ and $R=0.1I_m$. All other dynamics,
initial and noise laws, discount and terminal conventions are those of the
ten-state predecessor. Adjacent actuation and rank-one process noise are
explicit properties of this family, not a universal control benchmark.
The attainable reference uses backward Riccati recursion with independent
condensed block-matrix elimination and forward covariance checks. For each
action coordinate, the sum of the absolute sign coefficients bounds its
largest possible value over all $2^{40}$ initial/noise histories. Feasibility
is checked coordinate by coordinate, without summing different controls.

The excess-cost identity retains all off-diagonal curvature terms:
\[
 J(\pi)-J^*=\mathbb E\sum_{t=0}^{29}0.99^t
 (u_t+K_tx_t)^\top (R+0.99 B^\top P_{t+1}B)(u_t+K_tx_t).
\]
Small multi-action nonlinear policies are exhaustively enumerated to verify
the identity; every evaluated path also checks its telescoping equality,
including the realized noise martingale. The optimal cost is exact up to
floating-point reference checks. Neural-policy expectation is sampled.

\section{Frozen comparison and resources}
Seeds 10501--10503 are paired within each action dimension. Selection uses
1,024 IID validation paths with seed 1050001. All twelve paired procedures
are frozen before any outcomes on 16,384 holdout paths with seed 1050002
are computed. Those paths share initial/noise signs for paired contrasts.
Normal 95\% intervals measure conditional test-path uncertainty only; they
are neither finite-sample certificates nor intervals over optimizer seeds.
All results are retained, with no selected performance threshold or
outcome-dependent training extension.

Each method receives 77,824 transitions per pair. Four architecture proposals
receive 64 distillation updates and 2,048 SAC transitions before elimination;
two survivors receive another 2,048 transitions. The static search has equal
transition and component-gradient-minibatch work. Default results reuse its
best validation checkpoint from the default static member with actor and
critic widths [32,32] and learning rates 0.001. That member receives 19,456
transitions, so the default comparison has a smaller total tuning budget.
Equal component work does not imply equal FLOPs or wall time.

Across four dimensions and three paired seeds, the budget is 1,867,776
transitions, 463,104 SAC updates and 3,072 distillation updates, totaling
1,861,632 component-gradient minibatches. Four single-thread CPU workers
execute under a 90-minute, 16-GiB owned-RSS guard with a coordinator. These
small-network experiments do not require idle GPUs or establish GPU speedup.
Checkpoint contracts prevent cross-dimension restoration/replay/transfer.
Architecture mutation within a dimension retains the environment contract.
No oracle actions are used in training or distillation.
