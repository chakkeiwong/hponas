## Unchanged decision criteria

PASS requires all of the following:

1. At least 100 individual, unique mutants and every required module present.
2. Overall and each module kill score **at least 0.90**, with denominator
   `total - timeout - proved_equivalent`. Uncovered mutants stay in the denominator.
3. No unexcluded survivor in any critical function below.
4. At most five percent individually proved equivalent mutants. Only the twenty
   frozen lemmas above can establish an exclusion, by reconstructing each exact
   generated mutant. A name, approval, or descriptive label cannot prove
   behavioral equivalence. Unknown survivors remain in the score and veto.
5. At most twenty percent timeouts; no unchecked, suspicious, segfault, interrupted, skipped or type-check-only outcomes.
6. Fresh complete execution with identical source identity before, in the
   isolated copy, and after execution; V16 independently reconstructs the result.

A complete regular inventory violating scores, module coverage or critical
survivor requirements is FAIL. A wall limit, unavailable/misconfigured tool,
unchecked/abnormal results or timeout fraction above twenty percent is
INCONCLUSIVE. Neither can release the program. Partial runs preserve their
individual statuses; a timeout never becomes a kill.
