The audit answers two different questions. First, are the adopted mandatory
features implemented within their recorded scope? Second, does every original
aspiration exist as an unrestricted production capability? The evidence supports
the first within finite local scope, not the second. A failed research outcome,
watchlist or explicit exclusion is not an implemented feature.

\section{What the historical program actually promises now}

The lineage begins with the original survey/build proposal, continues through
the reset and repair program, then the PRD interface restoration, BANK model
profiles and GAP atomic closures. Later scope amendments replaced expensive
PPO empirical/default claims with self-contained finite known-answer checks and
narrowed operations to trusted supervised local research. The original
documents and failed attempts remain immutable evidence.

PBT, PB2-Mix, BG-PBT, DEHB and selected robust GP behavior progressed beyond
their old deferred labels. RGPE now includes an actual bounded surrogate
ensemble. In contrast, ifBO feasibility failed and integration was cancelled;
the watchlists in Chapter~\ref{sec:current-product} remain unimplemented.
The original 100-study/50-worker capacity is not established by eight-study/
four-worker local evidence. General posterior convergence, real-bank model
quality and arbitrary architecture search remain unestablished.

No newly unfulfilled mandatory \emph{core implementation} clause was found
within the adopted finite scope. This conclusion is bounded by the audit
method: unchanged source identities, retained independent per-clause oracles,
historical dispositions and focused semantic inspection, rather than new
formal verification of every line or a repeated broad campaign.

\section{Defects found and repaired in this extension}

The LaTeX was still a proposal and omitted current usage, compatibility and
support limits. Its Ray Tune substrate, pseudo-interfaces, old defaults and
calendar could mislead a user. This manual now leads with actual features and
executable examples, preserving the original text as clearly separated history.
A Markdown adapter guide also incorrectly claimed declared streaming was
unsupported; it now matches the implemented repeated-barrier contract. Current
support guidance points to the latest qualified semantic package rather than
only the older baseline.

Application evidence was too limited to explain realistic use. DOC-01 supplies
bounded response tuning and actual end-to-end trained neural coordinate search,
complete finite references and protected final outcomes. These repair the
demonstration/documentation gap; they do not close real-bank validation or
production-scale evidence gaps.

The extension exposed a governance constraint: GAP-01/FINAL-AUDIT must remain
the last master stage. Rather than weaken that guard, the master reopened its
terminal recheck after DOC-01's own final review. Earlier completed results
remain accepted historical evidence on their exact source. The extended master
can close only after the manual, examples, independent reconstruction,
rendering and governance checks pass, followed by that terminal recheck.
