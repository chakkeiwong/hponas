HPO-NAS 0.1.0 is an implemented Python toolkit for hyperparameter optimization
and moderate architecture-coordinate search. It combines search methods,
resource-aware evaluation, protected final scoring, persistence, recovery and
reporting through caller-owned model adapters. This manual describes available
features, supported method combinations, installed quick starts and practical
integration, with explicit limits on finite local qualification and production
claims. A complete historical feature audit distinguishes implemented behavior,
scope amendments, unsuccessful research outcomes and watchlists. Two executed,
self-contained applications demonstrate elastic-net logistic response HPO and
actual neural-network width/depth search for a manufactured equilibrium.
Exhaustive finite tuning references, retained fitted models, independent
numerical reconstruction and disjoint final tests make the examples auditable.
They reveal overhead and generalization limitations: finding the finite tuning
optimum does not guarantee the best final fit. The original survey and proposal
are preserved after the manual as clearly marked design history. Synthetic
evidence does not establish universal optimizer superiority, real-bank
usefulness, posterior convergence or production safety.
