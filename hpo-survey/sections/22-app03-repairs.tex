\section{Wallet: select the declared decision objective}
The model includes structural zeros, censoring, conditional variance and
optional incidence. The 24 configurations vary mean, scale, incidence and
regularization. Each is fit once on training observations. The selection score
is observed validation utility: capped wallet less fee, including a no-action
option. Model likelihood is reported as a comparator, not silently substituted
for utility. Final scoring uses the same frozen training-only model.
Across seeds 5301--5303 the selected-policy final utility differences relative
to likelihood selection are $-0.0000151980$, $-0.0018289500$ and $0.0013524612$.
This is mixed empirical performance, not demonstrated superiority.

Conditional on training, let $\widehat U_j$ be the mean of $n$ independent
MCAR validation utilities for fixed policy $j$, $j=1,\ldots,24$. Each utility
lies in the conservative interval $[-0.15,3]$. Hoeffding's inequality and a
union bound give simultaneous two-sided intervals of radius
\[
 r_\alpha=3.15\sqrt{\frac{\log(48/\alpha)}{2n}},\qquad
 U_j\in[\widehat U_j-r_\alpha,\widehat U_j+r_\alpha]\cap[-0.15,3].
\]
We report 90, 95 and 99 percent intervals. None of the selected policies is
certified above zero at 95 percent. The formula does not cover unbounded NLL,
dependent validation samples, adaptive refits or improvement over the comparator.

Under unknown MNAR observation, optimization cannot restore identification.
If observation probability is $0.75$, observed capped-wallet mean is $1$ and
the cap is $3$, the population mean has sharp bounds $[0.75,1.5]$.
Unobserved wallets all zero or all three give the same observed law but
different population means. Point identification requires additional assumptions.

\section{NAS: repair training, then confirm a frozen recipe}
The target is $f(x,a)=\exp(-ax)$ for $x\in[-0.5,0.5]$ and $a\in[0.2,0.6]$.
Two-layer SiLU networks use width four or eight and 81 training points.
Training geometries are random points including eight boundary points, or
a $9\times9$ tensor grid. Three recipes use 480 Adam steps at learning rate
$0.03$: raw loss, loss normalized by squared derivative limits, or normalized
loss followed by at most 80 L-BFGS iterations/100 evaluations.
The unchanged derivative limits are $0.12148729268184028$ for $x$ and
$0.0843661754735002$ for $a$.

Development seeds 6401 and 6402 and a $41\times41$ validation mesh choose
among jointly feasible recipes by parameter count, worst limit ratio, then
mean validation loss. Width four, tensor geometry and polishing are selected
(37 parameters). Confirmation seeds 7401--7406 each use an $81\times81$ mesh
and 512 fresh random points. All six pass both checks; worst mesh ratio is
$0.5753800514$. This does not prove a continuous-domain or all-seed guarantee.
NumPy derivatives independently reconstruct saved network scores.
An initial failed attempt rounded restored weights through float32; its source
and evidence are retained. The successor explicitly restores float64 tensors.
One failed fit is charged in addition to the successful roster.

\section{Transport: charge work and retain every comparison}
The banana target uses identity, exact and three frozen learned triangular
maps. Fixed HMC policies use step $0.15$ with length one, or independent
random lengths eight/twelve. Two fresh seeds and four chains per cell receive
8,192 gradient evaluations per chain, including 2,048 warmup evaluations.
Incomplete final work is retained as unused remainder. A trajectory of length
$L$ costs $L+1$ gradients. All 20 comparisons are reported; posterior
diagnostics do not rank native-inference candidates.
For stationary exact harmonic flow with independently refreshed momenta and
iid integration times, $\rho=\E[\cos T]$ and integrated autocorrelation time
is $(1+\rho)/(1-\rho)$. This analytic reference is not asserted for the
Metropolized leapfrog kernel. Basic split-$\widehat R$, 16-batch MCSE and ESS
are finite diagnostics, not rank-normalized diagnostics or convergence proofs.

\section{Policy: cross-fit under an explicit identification contract}
The synthetic MDP has three states, two actions and three steps. The repaired
policy class includes both threshold directions, with 48 configurations.
Two-fold cross-fitting shares nuisance fits only for identical fold/smoothing
inputs; final evaluation uses independent trajectories. Three fresh datasets
require 21 nuisance fits and 291 scores. Independent path enumeration
reconstructs doubly robust scores and all 512 deterministic Markov policy
values. Every selected policy equals the exact optimum $0.7601944790$.
The final estimates are $0.83612$, $0.88357$, $0.85316$, with descriptive iid
standard errors $0.07650$, $0.21066$, $0.06975$.
Known randomized logging and positive overlap are required. Unknown
identification or zero overlap is refused. The worlds $Y(a)=a$ and
$Y(a)=U$, $A=U$, share observed $Y=A$ but have ATE one and zero.
Observed data alone do not distinguish them; no real-bank recommendation follows.

\section{Forecast: respect information availability}
A ridge covariate model with linear Gaussian latent state varies lag one/two
and ridge $0.01/1$, with observation variance $0.4$. Two rolling folds train
on indices $[0,32)$ and $[0,48)$, scoring $[34,48)$ and $[50,64)$.
The final fold trains on $[0,72)$ and scores $[74,96)$.
Two embargo outcomes are masked while latent state propagates. Evaluation
is one-step sequential: each outcome updates state only after it is scored.
Independent full Gaussian conditioning and augmented ridge least squares
reconstruct all 27 fits. Future feature release, gap violations, entity overlap
and final-score exposure are refused. The grouped refusal is a separate
control, not proof of a jointly grouped-temporal workflow.
Final NLLs are $1.2568684$, $0.9817669$ and $1.3772477$; no universal
forecasting effectiveness claim is made.
